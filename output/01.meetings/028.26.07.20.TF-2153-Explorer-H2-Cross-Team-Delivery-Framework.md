## Executive Summary

While the core Explorer owning team (Terraform Visibility) maintains ultimate long-term feature accountability, two partner engineering teams are spinning up standalone workstreams to unlock delivery parallelization. This is to support the ambitious H2 roadmap goals established for Explorer and meet critical timelines.

---

## Key Technical Strategy & Risk Mitigation

Three core pillars to ensure autonomous delivery and eliminate cross-team blocking dependencies:

**1. Dedicated ETL Pipeline Migration**
The TF Visibility team will spearhead migrating ETL operations out of Rails and into an optimized, dedicated pipeline. This infrastructure is explicitly tailored for high-throughput ingestion into the Explorer DB, directly serving the goal of providing rich resource and policy visibility to customers.

**2. Shared Data Ingestion Foundations**
Both the Explorer and Actions teams require data transport from the `terraform-state-parser` into the Explorer DB. This shared requirement offers a high-leverage point for immediate, close technical collaboration. Building this baseline transport mechanism early unlocks downstream execution.

**3. Graph UI & Data Persistence Architecture**
The TF Actions team will own the graph side of the Explorer feature set, focusing on ingestion, persistence, and efficiency. The team will systematically leverage pre-existing Explorer DB records, utilizing database optimizations where applicable.

**4. Conversational Query**
The TF Partner Integrations team will own the Conversational Query experience for Explorer. The phase 1 solution is not UI-specific and is not a competing surface to Explorer chat. The conversational capability is treated as separate from where it lives, so it can converge with Explorer chat or other surfaces later.

2. **Shared KanBan JIRA board** — Ensures tracks and progress are transparent and aligned across teams. Teams maintain autonomy over their own backlog and agile rituals.
3. **Weekly status updates** — Shared in the standing EIC Integrations LT Sync across all three teams. Leadership uses these updates to share more widely.
4. **Cross-team kickoff** — EPD leaders across all teams will facilitate a kickoff to ensure all members are aware and aligned to the plan.
